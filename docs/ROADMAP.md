# Roadmap

Anchored to AfCEM 2026, Arusha, 11–13 November 2026.

## v0.5 — shipped
Professional directory with live filters (name, country, profession, specialty) and
role badges: Founder, Verified, Mentor, Editor, Moderator, Contributor, Country Rep.
Eight professional groups with join and leave, real member counts, group discussion
threads, and a member profile page for every member with direct messaging.

## v0.4 — shipped
Official brand content: vision, mission, purpose, values, promise, ambition and the
six core pillars. Media uploads to Supabase Storage with contributor permissions.
Community feed with posting, comments, likes, discussion rooms and a direct-message
inbox with real-time delivery.
